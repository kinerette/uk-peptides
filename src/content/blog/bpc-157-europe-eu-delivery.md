## What Is BPC-157?

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide — a 15-amino-acid sequence — derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice. It was first identified and characterised by researchers at the University of Zagreb in the early 1990s.

The compound is designated as a **research use only (RUO)** chemical and is legally available for laboratory and scientific research across the European Union.

### The published evidence base

BPC-157 has accumulated **over 100 peer-reviewed publications**, predominantly from Croatian, German, and American research groups. The breadth of investigated mechanisms is unusual for a synthetic peptide:

**Tissue repair and wound healing**
- Accelerated tendon-to-bone healing in rat models (Pevec et al., *Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc*, 2010)
- Enhanced skin wound closure via upregulation of growth hormone receptors
- Accelerated healing of transected Achilles tendons and ligaments in multiple rodent studies

**Gastrointestinal research**
- Cytoprotective effects in stomach ulcer models — the original discovery context
- Protective against NSAID-induced gut damage (aspirin, indomethacin) in rat studies
- Modulation of gut motility and enteric nervous system signalling

**Systemic and neurological effects**
- Observed effects on dopaminergic and serotonergic systems in rat models
- Attenuation of alcohol and opioid withdrawal symptoms in preclinical models
- Neuroprotective effects following traumatic brain injury in rodent research

**Muscle and skeletal research**
- Promotion of angiogenesis in ischaemic muscle models
- Enhanced recovery from crush injuries and surgical trauma
